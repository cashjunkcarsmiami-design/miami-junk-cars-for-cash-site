Another Vehicle Is Blocking Your Car in Hialeah: Prepare for Pickup

Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 29, 2026

Explain a blocked exit before selling a car in Hialeah. Coordinate access, the other driver’s availability and current photos before booking pickup.

Show the entire exit route

If you want to sell a junk car in Hialeah and another vehicle is parked in front of it, a photograph of the car you are selling is not enough to plan collection. From a safe position, show where it sits and the route to the exit. Explain whether the obstruction is inside the property, in a shared parking space or beside a gate. Do not assume the pickup driver can resolve it on arrival.

Coordinate with whoever can move the other car

Speak with the person responsible for the blocking vehicle and ask when they can move it. Do not promise an empty space if that depends on someone who has not confirmed their availability. If management approval is needed, arrange it before setting the visit. Do not move someone else’s vehicle or try to push the disabled car yourself to make room.

Update the buyer before departure

Tell the buyer when the route will be clear and what uncertainties remain. If the other vehicle returns or availability changes, give notice as soon as possible and ask whether to reschedule. Pickup included in an offer does not mean every obstruction can be resolved immediately. Also explain whether your car has keys, locked wheels or steering problems so the full collection conditions can be reviewed.

Before requesting an offer

Have the year, make, model, current condition, available documents and pickup area ready. Explain any details you do not yet know. Confirm the amount, payment conditions and whether collection is included before accepting.
