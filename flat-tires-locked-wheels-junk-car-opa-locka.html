Flat Tires or Locked Wheels on an Opa-locka Junk Car

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · September 21, 2026

“It does not run” and “it does not roll” are different details. When selling an unwanted vehicle in Opa-locka, wheel condition can affect how the pickup is planned even when the engine is already known to be inoperative. A clear description helps avoid an appointment with the wrong assumptions.

Report what you can see safely

Mention how many tires appear flat, whether any wheels are missing and whether collision damage is visible near a wheel. If the vehicle is sitting on blocks or has a damaged suspension, say so. Take photos only from a safe standing position; do not get beneath the car or disturb supports to improve a picture.

Do not test movement by pushing

A seller does not need to push a car into the street to establish whether it rolls. Tell the team its last known movement and whether you know of a locked wheel or steering issue. If you have not tried moving it, state that honestly. The pickup crew should decide what loading arrangements are appropriate.

Describe the surface and clearance

Tell the buyer whether the car is on pavement, gravel or soft ground, and whether another vehicle blocks access. Include slopes, narrow gates and nearby obstacles in the discussion. Wheel condition and parking position need to be considered together; a flat tire in an open driveway is not the same situation as a vehicle wedged against a wall.

Confirm what the purchase includes

Request an offer for the car with its current wheels and tires. Do not purchase replacements solely to make it easier to sell without first discussing the cost and necessity. Ask whether the agreed purchase includes the required pickup arrangements. Miami Junk Cars For Cash buys vehicles and does not sell replacement tires or provide a separate tire-repair service.
