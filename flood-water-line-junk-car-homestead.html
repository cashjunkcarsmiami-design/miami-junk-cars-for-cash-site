How to Document a Flood-Exposed Junk Car in Homestead

Water exposure can affect mechanical, electrical and interior components. Accurate photos and a clear history help the buyer evaluate the vehicle without unsafe testing.

Do not restart the vehicle

Avoid turning on a flood-exposed engine or electrical system. Stay away from moving water, downed lines and damaged high-voltage components. Use emergency authorities when the location remains dangerous.

Photograph evidence safely

Capture the exterior water line, seats, floor, dashboard and visible corrosion from stable ground. State whether the water was fresh, brackish or saltwater if known and how long the vehicle was exposed.

Describe pickup conditions

Explain mud, soft ground, blocked roads, locked wheels and whether the keys are available. Confirm that the site is accessible before scheduling.
