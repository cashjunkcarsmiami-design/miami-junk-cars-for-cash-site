What to have ready when you contact us

Give the year, make and model, explain whether the car runs, rolls and steers, and identify missing components. Tell us what ownership documents are available and where the vehicle is located. Label older photos so the discussion stays focused on the car’s current condition.

Before accepting, confirm the complete amount, payment method, included pickup, document requirements and collection window. Ask how reusable components and scrap metal may be handled for your particular vehicle; a purchase inquiry is not a promise about the final use of every part.
