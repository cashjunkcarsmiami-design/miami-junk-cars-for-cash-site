What Affects a Junk Car Cash Offer?

The year, make and model give a buyer the starting point. Newer vehicles and models with reusable parts may have more value, while older vehicles may be valued mainly for their remaining components and salvage material.

Condition also matters. Tell us whether the car runs, has accident damage, is missing wheels, has engine or transmission problems, was flooded, or has been sitting for a long time. Honest details and clear photos help produce a faster, more accurate offer.

  • Year, make, model and approximate mileage.
  • Running or non-running condition.
  • Accident, flood, engine or transmission damage.
  • Missing parts, tires, wheels or catalytic converter.
  • Vehicle location and towing access.
  • Title, registration and ownership information.
