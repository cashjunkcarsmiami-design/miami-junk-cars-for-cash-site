Photos That Help Explain a Kendall Junk Car

A complete photo set can answer condition and access questions before pickup.

Send PhotosKendall Service

Begin With Four Exterior Views

Photograph the front, rear and both sides in daylight when possible. Do not crop out wheels or damaged panels.

Document Specific Damage

Add close views of collision points, broken glass, engine damage, missing parts and the interior. Photograph all four tires when one or more are flat.

Identify the Vehicle Carefully

Share the year, make and model in writing. A VIN-label photo may help when appropriate, but send private identifying information only to the intended business contact.

Include the Parking Area

Step back and photograph the driveway, garage, gate or parking space. Show obstacles, surface conditions and room available around the vehicle.

Kendall Photo Questions

Are older photos sufficient?

Recent photos are better because stored vehicles can change condition over time.

Should personal documents appear in photos?

No. Remove or cover unrelated private information before sending images.
