Describing Engine Problems for a Junk Car Offer in Kendall

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · September 21, 2026

An accurate engine description does not require you to be a mechanic. For a junk car offer in Kendall, explain what happened, what was diagnosed and what remains unknown. The goal is to describe the vehicle as it is today, not to prove it can survive another trip.

Start with the last known running condition

Say when the vehicle last ran normally and what happened before it was parked. Useful descriptions include “the engine cranks but does not start,” “it stopped after overheating,” or “the shop said the engine needs replacement.” Keep an observed symptom separate from a confirmed diagnosis. A dashboard light alone does not identify every damaged component.

Mention repairs and disassembly

Tell the buyer whether a shop removed the engine, cylinder head or other major parts. Explain which loose components remain with the car and which are no longer available. An engine problem in a complete vehicle is different from a vehicle with the engine missing, so do not leave that detail until loading day.

Avoid starting it just for a video

Do not run an overheating, leaking or damaged engine solely to demonstrate a symptom. Existing photos and an honest description can support an initial review. If the buyer needs clarification, explain what you can safely provide. Do not attempt unfamiliar repairs or handle hot engine parts as part of the quoting process.

Request an as-is review

Provide the year, make, model, mileage if known and Kendall pickup location along with the engine description. Ask what the offer assumes and whether pickup is included in the agreed vehicle purchase. A car with an engine fault can be evaluated as a whole vehicle; no replacement engine purchase is required simply to ask for an offer.

What if I do not know the exact engine fault?

Say that the cause is unknown and describe the symptoms you observed. Share a shop's diagnosis only if one has actually been provided.
