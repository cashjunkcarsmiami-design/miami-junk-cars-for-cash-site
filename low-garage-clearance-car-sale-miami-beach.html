Selling a Car in a Low-Clearance Miami Beach Garage

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · September 28, 2026

The address of a garage does not tell the pickup team how to reach a parked car. If an unwanted vehicle is inside a Miami Beach building, discuss the route from the entrance to the space before confirming collection.

Gather the posted access information

Note the posted entrance clearance and the parking level if these are readily available. Describe ramps, sharp turns and controlled gates. If you do not know a measurement, say it is unknown and ask building management. Do not estimate clearance from a photo and present it as a confirmed dimension.

Explain whether the car can move

Tell the buyer whether the car starts, rolls and steers based on what you actually know. A car that cannot leave its parking space needs a different access review from one that can be brought to a suitable collection point. Do not attempt to tow or push it down a ramp yourself to meet an appointment.

Coordinate with the building

Ask the property contact about permitted pickup times and who can provide access. Share these limits with the buyer before choosing a window. Equipment suitability must be confirmed by the pickup team. Keep your quoted vehicle condition and the collection location clear so neither side assumes a curbside pickup when the car is upstairs.

Can a tow truck enter every garage?

Do not assume it can. The pickup team must review the entrance, route and vehicle condition before confirming an appropriate arrangement.
