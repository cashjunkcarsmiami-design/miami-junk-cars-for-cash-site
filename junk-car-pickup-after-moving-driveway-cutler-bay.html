Moving a Junk Car Before Pickup in Cutler Bay

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 10, 2026

A car quoted from photographs in one driveway may be collected from a different position later. In Cutler Bay, moving it behind another car, onto a lawn or to a neighbor's property can change the pickup plan even when the vehicle itself looks the same.

Describe the new position before moving the vehicle

Tell the buyer why you want to relocate the car and where it would end up. Explain the surface, surrounding vehicles and approach from the street. Ask whether the proposed position can be used for collection. Do not push a damaged or non-running car simply to create a better photo; its weight, steering and brakes may make the move unsafe.

Update photos from the collection approach

Once the car is in its final position, send an overall view from where the truck would approach, plus a view of the space around it. Describe whether it can roll and whether the tires hold air. These images answer a different question from damage photos: how the vehicle can be reached without assuming an ordinary driveway pickup.

Keep permission and timing connected

If the new position is on another person's property or shared parking, arrange access with the person who controls that space. Give the buyer the actual collection address and availability. Confirm the pickup arrangement again after the move, especially if a gate, another parked car or a temporary obstruction would prevent access at the original time.

Will the offer stay the same after I move the car?

It may, but ask before relying on that. A new location or difficult access can change collection arrangements. The buyer needs the final position and condition to confirm the offer and whether pickup is included.
