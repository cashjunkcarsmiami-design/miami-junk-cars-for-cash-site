Selling a Car With Suspension Damage in Cutler Bay

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 5, 2026

A car with a low corner, damaged wheel or suspension problem needs a different description from a car that simply will not start. When requesting a Cutler Bay junk car offer, explain the visible condition and movement limits so pickup can be reviewed before a visit.

Describe the affected corner

Identify which wheel or side is affected and whether the wheel appears tilted, displaced or missing. Explain how the damage happened if you know, but distinguish a visible observation from a diagnosis. A repair estimate can provide useful context without proving that every component listed is still on the vehicle. Tell the buyer whether the car has moved since the damage and where it currently sits.

Show the vehicle and its surroundings

Provide a recent overall photograph and a clear image of the affected side from a safe position. Include the driveway entrance and any nearby parked vehicles. The buyer needs to understand the space available, not only the damaged part. Do not arrange to drive the car to another address to simplify the sale; first describe the existing location and ask what collection options can be considered.

Agree on collection based on the current condition

Ask whether the offer includes pickup for the condition shown. Mention any missing wheels, loose parts or change in ride height before scheduling. If someone repairs, moves or dismantles the car after the quote, update the buyer. Confirm who can provide access and which paperwork is needed, and keep the vehicle accessible for the agreed collection plan.

Can I request an offer without a repair diagnosis?

Yes. Describe what you can observe and share recent photos. Tell the buyer that the exact mechanical fault has not been confirmed.
