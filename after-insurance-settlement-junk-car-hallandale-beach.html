Selling a Damaged Car After an Insurance Claim in Hallandale Beach

An insurance payment or claim decision does not always mean the vehicle is immediately available for private sale. Confirm who owns and controls the car before accepting an offer.

Read the settlement and title instructions

Determine whether the insurer took ownership, issued a salvage instruction or requires additional documents. Contact the insurer or a qualified professional when the settlement language is unclear.

Confirm physical release

If the vehicle is at a shop, tow yard or storage property, ask what authorization and charges must be resolved. Do not send a buyer until the property confirms release.

Disclose damage and status

Share collision damage, missing parts, keys, wheel condition and current title status accurately. Keep copies of insurer, storage and sale documents.
