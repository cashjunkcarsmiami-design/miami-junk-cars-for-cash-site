Selling a Collision-Damaged SUV in Cutler Bay

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · September 21, 2026

A damaged SUV needs more than a description such as “it had an accident.” Before requesting cash for a junk vehicle in Cutler Bay, separate what you can see from what a repair shop has confirmed. That gives the buyer a clearer basis for an offer and helps the pickup team plan safely.

Describe the damage without guessing

State which side was hit and whether doors, wheels or glass are visibly damaged. Mention deployed airbags, leaking fluid and any parts that have already been removed. If you have a shop's diagnosis, summarize it as a diagnosis; if you do not know whether the engine works, say it is unknown. An exterior photo cannot establish hidden structural or mechanical condition.

Explain where the SUV is now

A vehicle at home, a repair facility and a secured storage lot can require different arrangements. Give the actual pickup location when scheduling and identify who controls access. Resolve any release requirements with the property or facility before the appointment. A pickup appointment by itself does not authorize removal from someone else's lot.

Do not test-drive it for a quote

You do not need to prove that a collision-damaged SUV can drive by taking it onto the road. Describe its last known running condition, whether keys are available and any visible wheel damage. Do not handle deployed airbag components, leaking fluids or exposed wiring. Leave questions about loading equipment to the pickup team.

Confirm the complete-vehicle offer

Ask what condition the offer assumes, what documents must be reviewed and whether the agreed vehicle purchase includes pickup. List loose vehicle parts that will accompany the SUV. Miami Junk Cars For Cash buys unwanted vehicles; this is not an order for replacement parts or a standalone towing booking.

Do I need a repair estimate before requesting an offer?

You can request an initial review using the details you know. Share an existing diagnosis if available, but do not invent one or buy repairs solely to obtain a quote.
