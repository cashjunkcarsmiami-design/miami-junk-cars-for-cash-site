Advertising Magnets Before Selling a Business Car in Wynwood

Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 29, 2026

Review removable advertising magnets and business material before selling a vehicle in Wynwood, and disclose any changes to its condition.

Identify what is removable

A business car in Wynwood may carry magnets, loose signs and promotional materials you do not want to hand over. Make a list before requesting an offer, distinguishing these objects from an attached vinyl wrap or permanent modification. The initial photograph should show the actual vehicle, but explain which business items will be removed. Do not offer equipment that belongs to a supplier or someone else.

Show the surface that will remain visible

If you safely remove a magnet, take a current photograph of the area it covered. If color differences or damage appear, disclose them without claiming a cause you do not know. Do not use an old advertising photo as evidence of current condition or spend on repairs assuming they will increase the offer. If something does not come off easily, discuss how to describe it before trying to force it loose.

Leave an inventory with the handoff contact

Tell the buyer what you will keep and what stays installed on the vehicle. Share the same inventory with the person holding the keys and providing access. If the car still displays outdated contact information, separately provide the current coordination number. Before confirming pickup, check that the offer applies to the final configuration and that the parking area will be available at the agreed time.

Pickup planning checklist for Wynwood

Updated October 30, 2026. Review these details with the buyer before confirming collection.

  • Inventory removable advertising magnets separately from painted graphics, wraps and permanent vehicle components. Tell the buyer which items the business plans to retain before agreeing to a vehicle description.
  • Ask the responsible business contact to remove retained materials safely under company procedures. Do not promise that underlying paint is undamaged if it has not been inspected or if an area remains covered.
  • If the graphics or accessories change after the quote, send current pictures and ask whether the offer needs review. Keep business contact information in the sale conversation accurate even if an old number remains on the bodywork.
