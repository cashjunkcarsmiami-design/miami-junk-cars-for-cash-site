Selling a Car With a Custom Wrap in Wynwood

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · September 28, 2026

A vinyl wrap or business graphic can change how a vehicle looks without showing its underlying condition. If you are selling a wrapped car in Wynwood, explain what is visible, what you know about the original panels and which changes you plan before handoff.

Describe the wrap separately

Tell the buyer whether the vehicle has a full wrap, partial graphics or removable business lettering. Mention lifting edges, covered damage or replacement panels you know about. Do not claim that the paint underneath is undamaged unless you have a reliable basis. Use current photos and explain that the finish visible in them is a covering rather than the original paint.

Agree on what stays

If you intend to remove graphics or equipment, explain that before requesting a final offer. Removal can change the visible finish and should not be treated as an automatic increase in value. Discuss company branding with the person responsible for it. The vehicle quote should describe the car in the condition that will actually be handed over.

Include mechanical condition

A distinctive exterior does not replace information about the engine, transmission, mileage and missing components. List those details even if the photos focus on the wrap. If the car is kept at a studio or shared commercial lot, coordinate access with the property contact and identify the correct vehicle when arranging pickup.
