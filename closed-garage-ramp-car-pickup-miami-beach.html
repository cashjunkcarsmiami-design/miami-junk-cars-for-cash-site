The Garage Ramp Is Closed: Coordinating a Car Pickup in Miami Beach

Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 29, 2026

Ask about authorized alternative access and restrictions if a garage ramp is closed before selling a car in Miami Beach.

Check the route intended for vehicles

If your car is inside a Miami Beach garage and the ramp is temporarily closed, explain that before booking collection. Being able to reach the parking space on foot does not mean the car has an available exit. Describe the level, space and known closure. Do not assume another entrance can accommodate collection simply because residents are allowed to use it.

Ask the property manager for access information

Find out when the route is expected to reopen, whether an authorized alternative exists and what height or time restrictions apply. Share this as information provided by management, without personally guaranteeing that collection equipment can enter. Do not remove barriers or use restricted areas to help the vehicle leave. If access remains unresolved, leave the visit unconfirmed until the route can be reviewed.

Reconfirm if work or access hours change

Before collection, verify with the building contact that the agreed route remains available. Share changes and ask whether the appointment should be rescheduled. Include vehicle details such as missing keys or locked wheels, because an alternative exit may need a different assessment. Confirm what collection the offer includes, and do not move the car onto a closed ramp or attempt to bring it down yourself.

Pickup planning checklist for Miami Beach

Updated October 30, 2026. Review these details with the buyer before confirming collection.

  • Confirm whether the ramp is closed to all vehicles or restricted at particular times. Ask the building’s authorized contact about the permitted collection route instead of assuming a pedestrian entrance can serve the truck.
  • Give the buyer the parking level, current car position, known clearance limits and the contact who controls access. Explain whether the car can roll or steer, and identify anything you have not checked.
  • Request a collection plan based on the actual garage restrictions. Do not direct the operator through a closed ramp or move an unsafe car to another level yourself. Reconfirm access if building arrangements change.

Have the year, make, model, current condition, available documents and pickup area ready. Explain any details you do not yet know. Confirm the amount, payment conditions and whether collection is included before accepting.
