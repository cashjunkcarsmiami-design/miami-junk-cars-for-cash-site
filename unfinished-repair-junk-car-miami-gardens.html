Selling a Junk Car After an Unfinished Repair in Miami Gardens

A disassembled vehicle at a shop or home needs a complete parts and access description. Resolve any repair bill, storage or possession issue before arranging removal.

Confirm release from the repair location

Ask the shop or property holder what must be resolved before the vehicle can leave. Do not schedule pickup while a lien, bill, authorization or possession dispute is unresolved.

List removed and loose components

Identify missing wheels, engine parts, battery, catalytic converter, body panels and interior pieces. Explain which parts belong to the vehicle and whether they can be transported.

Plan safe loading

Report whether the car rolls, steers and has keys. Keep loose parts organized, but do not reinstall heavy components or work under an unsupported vehicle.
