Picking Up an Accident-Damaged Car From a Body Shop

Release authorization and an accurate parts list should be settled before dispatch.

CallLocal Service

Confirm the Shop Will Release It

Ask the facility about authorization, outstanding balance questions, pickup hours and whether the owner must appear. Resolve these matters directly with the shop.

List Accident and Repair Status

Explain deployed airbags, suspension damage, broken glass and whether panels, wheels or mechanical parts were removed during inspection.

Identify Loose Components

Ask which removed parts belong with the car and how they are stored. Photograph loose items and confirm whether they are included in the transaction.

Give the Correct Shop Contact

Provide the person responsible for vehicle release and the approved pickup window. Keep both parties informed if access or timing changes.

Body Shop Questions

Can the buyer resolve a repair-shop balance?

Do not assume so. The owner and facility must clarify balances and release conditions.

Why list deployed airbags?

They help describe collision severity and the vehicle's current condition.
