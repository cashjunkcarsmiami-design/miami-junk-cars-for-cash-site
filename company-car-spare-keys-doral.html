Spare Keys When Selling a Company Car in Doral

Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 29, 2026

Coordinate spare keys, vehicle labels and handoff responsibilities when selling a company car in Doral, keeping business access items separate.

Locate the key sets without mixing vehicles

At a Doral business, the main car key may be at reception while a spare is in another office. Before requesting collection, ask who holds each set and verify which vehicle it belongs to. An old fleet label is not enough to identify a car. Compare the year, make, model and photographs of the vehicle being offered, and say if any key has not yet been checked.

Separate business access from vehicle accessories

A key ring may include office, warehouse or gate keys that are not part of the sale. Review it with the responsible person before handing it over. Do not send detailed photographs of keys, codes or internal labels through a public channel. If a remote provides access to business premises, follow the company’s procedure for removing or returning it and arrange an authorized alternative for collection.

Leave instructions with the handoff contact

Record who will hold the confirmed keys, who can provide access and whom the buyer should call. If shifts change, pass along the same summary and do not promise someone will be present without checking. Explain any missing keys before accepting an offer rather than assuming they will turn up on pickup day. Also confirm outstanding paperwork, payment conditions and collection at the vehicle’s current location.

Before requesting an offer

Have the year, make, model, current condition, available documents and pickup area ready. Explain any details you do not yet know. Confirm the amount, payment conditions and whether collection is included before accepting.
