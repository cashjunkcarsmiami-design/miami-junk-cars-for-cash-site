Missing Keys? Requesting a Junk Car Offer in North Miami

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · September 21, 2026

Missing keys do not describe the entire pickup problem. A North Miami vehicle may be unlocked but unable to shift, or locked with belongings still inside. Explain each issue when requesting an offer so the buyer can decide what is possible before an appointment is made.

Explain which keys are missing

Say whether all keys are missing or a spare is available elsewhere. Identify whether it is a traditional key or a key fob if you know. Also mention whether the doors and trunk can be opened normally. Do not assume that an unlocked door means the steering or transmission can be operated.

Keep access and ownership separate

A missing key is an access issue; missing ownership documents are a different question. Tell the buyer what documents you have and ask what must be reviewed. Having physical access to a vehicle does not by itself establish permission to sell it. Do not arrange removal of someone else's car just because you can reach it.

Do not force the ignition or shifter

Do not break a window, bypass a lock or force a transmission control to prepare for pickup. Describe the last known condition and let the team assess loading needs. If you cannot retrieve belongings safely, explain that before confirming the handoff. Ask whether the proposed pickup can accommodate the exact vehicle and parking position.

Ask before spending money on a replacement key

A replacement key can be costly, and its effect on a junk car offer is not guaranteed. Request a review of the vehicle as it stands before ordering one solely for the sale. Include the year, make, model, condition and North Miami pickup area. Confirm whether keys or access changes are needed before the appointment and whether they affect the offer.

En español

Si perdiste las llaves de tu carro en North Miami, explica si se pueden abrir las puertas y qué documentos tienes. No fuerces el encendido ni la palanca. Consulta las condiciones de compra y recogida antes de pagar una llave nueva.

Should I buy a replacement key before calling?

Ask first. Whether a replacement is useful depends on the vehicle, access and pickup plan; it does not automatically improve the offer enough to cover its cost.
