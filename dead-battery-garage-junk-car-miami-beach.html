Do not assume a jump start solves everything

A battery may be only one problem. Report warning lights, collision or flood exposure, damaged cables and how long the vehicle has been parked. Avoid repeated starting attempts when damage is suspected.

Document garage access

Provide clearance height, floor level, ramp turns, space width and security procedures. Mention whether another car must move before loading.

Explain electronic restrictions

Tell dispatch if doors, steering, parking brake or transmission remain locked. Do not force electronic releases or tow points without vehicle-specific guidance.
