Arranging Junk Car Pickup From a Doral Office Parking Lot

A disabled employee or company vehicle in an office lot may require approval from property management before an outside truck can enter.

Call (305) 316-4221WhatsApp

Identify the responsible parties

Confirm who owns the vehicle, who manages the lot and who will meet the driver. Management permission alone does not authorize a vehicle sale.

Choose an approved pickup window

Ask about vendor check-in, loading zones and times with less employee or delivery traffic. Provide the building, entrance, floor or space number and security contact.

Report access and vehicle condition

Mention covered parking, height limits, flat tires, missing keys, locked steering and nearby cars. Keep pedestrians away from the loading path.
