Gather every available record

Verify requirements before pickup

Document rules vary by situation. Contact the appropriate Florida agency or a qualified professional when ownership, estate, lien or signature questions remain. Do not alter records or sign another person’s name.

Prepare the vehicle details

Provide the VIN, condition, location, keys and access restrictions. Keep copies of completed transaction and transfer records after the sale.
