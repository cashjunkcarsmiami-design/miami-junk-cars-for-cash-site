Trees and Overhead Access Around a Homestead Junk Car

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 10, 2026

A long-parked Homestead car can be reachable on foot while still difficult for a collection truck to approach. Trees, low branches, carports or overhead structures belong in the pickup conversation. A photo of the car alone may not show the available approach.

Photograph the route as well as the car

Send a view from the property entrance toward the vehicle and another view showing the surrounding space. Describe visible branches or a covered area without guessing a safe clearance. Tell the buyer whether another route is available. The truck's requirements need confirmation from the pickup team rather than an estimate based on the seller's everyday vehicle.

Do not alter overhead hazards for a pickup

If branches, cables or structures seem to interfere, report them and ask whether collection can be planned around the current setup. Do not climb, cut near utility lines or move damaged structures to make room for the truck. If access cannot be confirmed, resolve the property issue through an appropriate professional before arranging a different collection time.

Keep ground condition in the same discussion

Overhead space is only part of access. Also describe whether the car sits on pavement, soil or another surface, whether wheels are present and whether it can roll. Tell the buyer about a recent change such as rain-softened ground. Confirm the final location, access and included collection together so the driver is not relying on an outdated view of the property.

Does a wide driveway guarantee truck access?

No. The approach, overhead space, surface and vehicle position all matter. Send current views and let the buyer confirm whether pickup can be arranged. Do not assume a truck will fit because a passenger car can enter.
