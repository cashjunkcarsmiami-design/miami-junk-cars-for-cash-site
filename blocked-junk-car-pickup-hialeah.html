What to Do When a Junk Car Is Blocked in Hialeah

Dispatch should know about blocked access before the truck arrives. Moving one vehicle in advance can turn a difficult pickup into a routine one.

Show the entire parking arrangement

Send photos that include the junk car, surrounding vehicles, gate and street—not only close-ups of damage. Explain which vehicles can be moved and who has their keys.

Confirm a clear route

Measure narrow gates and identify low branches, fences, posts or sharp turns. Never use a neighbor’s driveway or private property without permission.

Avoid pushing without a plan

A car with locked steering, seized brakes or flat tires may not move safely by hand. Let the pickup professional evaluate the position and required equipment.
