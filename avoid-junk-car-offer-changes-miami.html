How to Avoid Unexpected Changes to a Junk Car Offer

A clear description, useful photos and complete pickup terms reduce misunderstandings.

Call for an OfferSend PhotosMiami Service

Describe More Than the Visible Damage

State whether the engine starts, transmission engages, steering unlocks and wheels roll. List missing keys, battery, catalytic converter, wheels, seats or body panels. Explain previous fire, flood or collision damage instead of relying on photos alone.

Photograph the Vehicle Before Pickup

Keep recent front, rear, side, interior and VIN-label photos. Include damaged areas and the space surrounding the car. These provide a shared reference for the condition described when the offer was discussed.

Confirm Ownership and Access

Ask which ownership documents must be reviewed. Provide the type of property, surface, gate restrictions and parking position so towing requirements are known beforehand.

Review the Complete Terms

Confirm whether towing is included, when payment occurs and which specific conditions could change the amount. Keep the agreed details until the vehicle and documents have been transferred.

Offer Questions

Can undisclosed missing parts affect an offer?

Yes. Identify all known missing or damaged components before accepting the amount.

Why keep the original photos?

They document the vehicle condition shared when the quote was prepared.
