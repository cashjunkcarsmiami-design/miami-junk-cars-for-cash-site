How to Compare Junk Car Offers Beyond the Dollar Amount

The highest number is not always the clearest final offer. Compare what is included, what can change and when payment occurs before choosing a buyer.

Call (305) 316-4221WhatsApp

Ask what the quote assumes

Confirm the year, make, model, VIN, condition, missing parts, title status and location used for the estimate. A quote based on incomplete information may change when the vehicle is inspected.

Compare the complete transaction

Ask whether pickup is included, whether any fees apply, how payment is made and who handles transfer documents. Get important terms in writing when possible.

Check the pickup commitment

Confirm the time window, access requirements and what happens if the driver finds a condition difference. Do not release the car until final terms, payment and paperwork are understood.
