Sending Junk Car Details by WhatsApp in Miami

By Miami Junk Cars For Cash · October 5, 2026

A clear message can save several rounds of questions when you want to sell an unwanted car in Miami. Send enough information to identify the vehicle and understand pickup needs, then ask which details still need confirmation before an offer is accepted.

Start with one complete vehicle description

Put the year, make, model and pickup city in your first message. Add whether the car starts, drives or has been parked, and describe the most recent condition you actually know. If there are two vehicles, separate their details and label the photos so an offer for one is not mistaken for an offer for both. Mention whether keys and ownership documents are available without sending private identification in the opening message.

Use photographs that answer different questions

Send an overall view, the visible damage and a view showing where the car is parked. A close-up of a dent does not show whether the truck can reach the vehicle. Use recent images and point out anything that has changed since they were taken. You do not need to photograph sensitive documents or people to explain vehicle condition. Ask for a specific additional angle if the buyer cannot assess an issue.

Confirm what happens after the message

A WhatsApp conversation is a quote inquiry until the offer and pickup arrangement are confirmed. Ask what information the offer depends on, which vehicle components are included and whether collection is included. Keep the agreed amount and scheduling details in the same conversation. If the location or condition changes before pickup, send an update instead of relying on the earlier photographs.

Does sending photos reserve a pickup?

No. Ask for explicit confirmation of the offer, collection address and scheduling arrangements before treating a pickup as booked.
